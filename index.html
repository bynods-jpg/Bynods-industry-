About us

Bynods Industry is a clothing manufacturer based in Sialkot, Pakistan. We make garments for clothing brands, clubs, teams, schools and retailers, from one custom line to a full collection.

You bring the design, the sketch or just an idea. We handle pattern, fabric, cutting, printing, stitching, finishing and packing, so what reaches you is ready to sell or ready to wear.

Common questions

Yes. Send a tech pack, sketch, photo or a sample garment and we will develop it.

Yes. We make a sample for your approval before bulk production.
